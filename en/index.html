Group C 2026 and Loksevak AI: common questions

By the corrigendum of 6 November 2026, 16,717 posts across 27 cadres. The largest: Clerk-Typist (3,966), Staff Nurse (3,911), Village Revenue Officer or Talathi (1,840), Craft Instructor (1,388) and Assistant Agriculture Officer (1,203). The full list is on the Group C 2026 page.

Sunday, 24 January 2027. It was first set for 3 January 2027; the corrigendum of 6 November moved it. Mains dates will be announced separately after the prelims result.

New applications and options for the new cadres run from 2 PM on 13 November 2026 to 11:59 PM on 27 November 2026, only at mpsconline.gov.in. Fees are paid online (card, net banking, UPI) by 30 November; the challan option is closed. Candidates who already applied pay nothing extra for the new cadres; they only submit their options.

Most posts need a degree in any discipline. Clerk-Typist and Tax Assistant also need the typing qualification; Assistant Motor Vehicle Inspector needs an automobile or mechanical diploma, experience and a licence; Staff Nurse needs GNM or B.Sc Nursing; Civil Engineering Assistant needs a civil diploma or degree. The upper age limit is relaxed by one year this year. Post-wise details on the Group C 2026 page.

100 questions, 100 marks, 60 minutes, objective. A quarter mark is deducted for every wrong answer. Under the MPSC five-option system an unattempted question must have the fifth option marked, or it is deducted as well. The prelims is qualifying only; final selection rests on the Mains. Every set and live test in the app is scored by this exact rule.

Every Sunday at 11 AM, one paper for all of Maharashtra in the prelims pattern. Everyone starts and finishes together. The moment it ends you see your marks, the cut-off comparison and your rank in the state, district and taluka. Register in the app; registered students hear the date of the first statewide paper first.

Everyone has a different pace and method, so the plan has to differ too. A short diagnostic test at the start shows your level in each subject. The daily plan is built from it: weak subjects first, strong ones on revision only. Every mistake is logged with its cause and the next practice changes accordingly. No two students get the same plan.

In Pune, Nashik and Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar, for students on the Super Pack. The sessions are run by officers who cleared MPSC and by subject experts, in small groups, built from your live test results. Seats are limited; dates and venues reach registered students on WhatsApp.

The account is free and needs no card; everything in Plus is free for the first 24 hours. Right now it runs in the browser at app.loksevak.ai, on phones too. The Android and iPhone apps arrive soon on Google Play and the App Store, with the same login and the same progress.
