What the corrigendum of 6 November changed

  • A big rise in vacancies: From 5,707 to 16,717 posts, with 22 new cadres: among them Talathi (1,840), Staff Nurse (3,911), Craft Instructor (1,388), Assistant Agriculture Officer (1,203) and Civil Engineering Assistant (934 + 949).
  • A new exam date: The combined prelims now takes place on Sunday, 24 January 2027 instead of 3 January 2027.
  • One year of age relaxation: By the government resolution of 5 November 2026, the upper age limit is relaxed by one year.
  • Existing applicants: Candidates who already applied do not apply or pay again for the new cadres; they submit options from My Account at mpsconline.gov.in.
  • Options are mandatory: An option not submitted within the window (by 27 November 2026) will not be accepted at any later stage.
  • Online fees only: Fees are paid online only; the bank challan facility is withdrawn.

Eligibility in brief

Education

Most posts need a degree in any discipline from a recognised university. Technical posts need the diploma or degree of that branch (see the table above).

Typing

Clerk-Typist and Tax Assistant need the typing qualification (Marathi 30 or English 40 wpm) along with the degree, as in paragraph 3.3.5 of the original advertisement. A typing skill test is part of the selection.

Age

Age limits as in the original advertisement, with a one-year relaxation in the upper limit this year. Age is reckoned on 1 October 2026 for the original cadres and on the last date of application, 27 November 2026, for the newly added cadres (Annexure 4).

Assistant Motor Vehicle Inspector

An automobile or mechanical engineering diploma (or a recognised equivalent), one year of experience in a motor vehicle workshop, a valid licence (geared motorcycle, light motor vehicle, transport vehicle), height and chest or weight standards, and no colour blindness.

Staff Nurse

GNM diploma, B.Sc Nursing or Post Basic B.Sc Nursing, with registration at the Maharashtra Nursing Council, as the advertisement states.

Reservation for persons with disabilities

Which disability categories qualify for which post is set out in Annexure 2 of the corrigendum. The Assistant Motor Vehicle Inspector post is marked as not suitable.

Prelims pattern

¼negative marking: 0.25 marks off per wrong answer
  • Format: Objective, multiple choice; offline OMR; the paper is in Marathi and English.
  • The fifth option: If you leave a question unattempted you must mark the fifth option on the OMR sheet; a question with nothing marked is deducted as well.
  • Screening only: The prelims qualifies you for the Mains; its marks do not count in the final merit list.
  • The Mains: Cadre-wise; Clerk-Typist and Tax Assistant also have a typing skill test. Details in the original advertisement.
  • In Loksevak AI: Every practice set and live test is scored by this exact rule: net score, fifth option included.

Prelims syllabus: seven subjects

1Current affairsWorld and India, with special weight on Maharashtra.
2Civics and polityThe Constitution of India at an introductory level, state administration, rural administration (Panchayati Raj) and urban administration.
3HistoryModern India, with special reference to Maharashtra.
4Geography (with special reference to Maharashtra)The earth, world regions, climate, latitude and longitude, soil types of Maharashtra, rainfall, major crops, cities, rivers, industries and population.
5EconomyThe Indian economy: national income, agriculture, industry, foreign trade, banking, population, poverty and unemployment, monetary and fiscal policy. Government finance: budget, accounts, audit. The economy of Maharashtra.
6General sciencePhysics, chemistry, zoology, botany and hygiene.
7Reasoning and arithmeticReasoning questions that test how quickly and accurately a candidate thinks; arithmetic with addition, subtraction, multiplication, division, decimals and fractions, percentages, ratio and proportion, time, work and speed.

How to apply and submit options

Already applied?

  1. Log in at mpsconline.gov.in and open My Account.
  2. Against advertisement 017/2026, click View, then Recheck Eligibility.
  3. Tick the new cadres you qualify for in the right-hand column, then Apply and Submit.
  4. No fee again. The claims in your original application stand and cannot be changed.
  5. Options only until 11:59 PM on 27 November 2026; later requests are not accepted.

Applying for the first time?

  1. Create a profile at mpsconline.gov.in (or log in).
  2. In Online Application, click Check Eligibility against advertisement 017/2026.
  3. Choose the cadres you qualify for and fill the form; upload documents as the original advertisement requires.
  4. Pay online by debit or credit card, net banking or UPI by 11:59 PM on 30 November 2026.
  5. Fee as in the original advertisement: ₹394 unreserved; ₹294 backward classes, EWS, PwD and orphans; ₹44 ex-servicemen. Verify before paying.

Applications go only through the Commission's official website. Loksevak AI does not take applications and is not connected with the Commission.
