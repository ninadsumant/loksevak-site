What does a Civil Engineering Assistant do?

A technical post assisting engineers on roads, buildings and bridges (Public Works) and on canals, dams and irrigation works (Water Resources): site supervision, measurements, estimates and records.

Eligibility and application

Education

A diploma or degree in Civil Engineering (Annexure 3). For the Public Works Department, qualifications the government treats as equivalent are also accepted.

Age

For both cadres, age is reckoned on the last date of application, 27 November 2026 (Annexure 4); the upper limit is relaxed by one year.

Application

Both departments can be chosen in one application; Water Resources posts are listed circle-wise (Annexure 1).

Selection stages

  1. Combined prelims, 24 January 2027
  2. Mains: cadre-wise
  3. Document verification and posting by department or circle

What is different about preparing for this post?

  • Two departments make 1,883 posts; choose both in your options and double the chance.
  • General studies and Marathi are the prelims challenge for diploma holders; the technical part comes in the Mains.
  • Water Resources posts are circle-wise, so check both the posts in your circle and your district rank in the live test.
