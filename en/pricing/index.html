A live test is not just a paper

In other apps a test series is a way to sell the class's course. Here the test is the starting point, and the four steps after it are the real studying.

1The paper, Sunday 11 AM

With the whole state at one time, in the prelims pattern, negative marking and fifth option included.

2Detailed analysis the same instant

Marks, state, district and taluka rank, and the distance to the cut-off. The cause of every mistake: haste, a weak concept or a guess. Weak links by subject and by topic, made plain.

3A week built on the weak topics

Your daily study plan changes by itself: weak topics first, wrong answers into smart flashcards, strong subjects on revision only. The AI mentor answers on those topics, with sources.

4Measure the gain next Sunday

A new paper, a new rank, and clear proof whether last week's weak topics are now strong. The same cycle for 10 weeks, up to the exam.

Not a coaching class's app

There is no coaching class behind Loksevak AI and no course to sell. The live test is the product; the analysis and the studying after it happen in the app, with the free tools. Nobody calls you about a course after the paper.

One test or the series, paid once. No subscription, nothing charged automatically. UPI, debit or credit card, or net banking.

One test, the series or the Super Pack: once paid, nothing is refunded for any reason and a registration cannot be cancelled. A missed test is not refunded either, so register only after checking the Sunday date and the 11 AM start.
