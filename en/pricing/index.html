Payment, subscription and refund rules, in plain words

24-hour free trial

Everything in Plus is free for 24 hours from your first OTP login. No bank card is needed and nothing is charged automatically. After 24 hours the account moves to the free plan by itself.

Subscription and cancellation

Plans are bought in the mobile app or at app.loksevak.ai. A plan bought through the Play Store or App Store is cancelled from that store's subscription settings; one bought on the web is cancelled from the subscription section in the app. We notify you before a term ends. Cancelling midway keeps your features active until the last day of the paid term.

100% refund within 7 days

Within the first 7 days of a subscription you can ask for a full refund, no reason needed, through the app store or by email to support@loksevak.ai. The money returns to the original payment method within 5 to 7 working days.

Super Pack reservation and refund

Offline seats are limited, so a seat is reserved first and paid for once confirmed. Cancel before the first offline session for a 100% refund.

Fee changes

A change in subscription fees never affects your current plan. We tell you at least 30 days before the next renewal.

Taxes

All prices on the website are in Indian rupees (INR) and include the applicable GST.
